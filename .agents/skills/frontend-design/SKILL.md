# Frontend Design - Anthropic Official AI Agent Design Skill

## Trigger Conditions
Triggers whenever the AI Agent crafts new screen interfaces, custom buttons, badges, theme cards, or visual presentation components.

## Design Directives

### 1. Bold Aesthetic Choices
- Avoid safe, generic light-gray templates.
- Embrace rich contrast themes: Gold & Carbon, Slate & Cyan, Emerald & Platinum.

### 2. High-Impact Animations & Transitions
- Use 180ms hover scale animations on primary action buttons.
- Use 300ms 3D Y-axis rotation matrix transforms for card flipping.
- Provide clear visual toast notifications (`CustomToast`) upon user actions.

### 3. Professional Card Layout Principles
- Real physical business cards require balanced alignment: Company Header top, Personal Bio middle, Contact Info & Badges bottom.
- Action triggers belong in a dedicated floating toolbar outside the card preview.
